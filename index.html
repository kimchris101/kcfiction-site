What I write

Six flavors of the same obsession.

I keep landing in the same territory from different angles. Here's the map of the places my writing tends to end up.

🔀

Branching Narratives

Stories that fork, weave and reunite. Every choice a reader makes writes a new version of the ending.

🌍

Living Worlds

Deeply detailed settings — languages, factions, weather patterns — that keep breathing after the page ends.

📖

Serial Fiction

Weekly chapters posted with cliffhangers designed to make you check back Monday morning.

🎭

Character Studies

Slow, honest portraits of people who feel more like neighbors than characters.

☕

Micro-fiction

Tiny 100-word stories written in coffee shop margins. Small enough to fit, sharp enough to stay.

🎲

Interactive Prompts

Writing games and prompts you can jump into — no experience needed, just curiosity.

About

A hobby I take seriously.

I've been writing since middle school — mostly for me, sometimes for a small circle of readers who keep asking "what happens next?" I love stories that don't sit still: choose-your-path fiction, worlds you can wander, and characters that surprise you. This page is a home for those experiments.
